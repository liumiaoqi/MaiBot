=== x63: memory kernel <-> decay tail (symbolic) ===

--- 1. Markov kernel K(t) = g*delta(t) ---
mhat(s) = m0/(s + g)  ->  m(t) = m0*e^{-g t}  (pure exponential)

--- 2. power-law kernel K(t) = c*t^-alpha (0<alpha<1), Laplace transform ---
LaplaceTransform[c t^-alpha] = c*s^(-1 + alpha)*Gamma[1 - alpha]

mhat(s) = m0/(s + K^(s)); as s->0, s^(alpha-1) dominates s
=> mhat(s) ~ m0/(c Gamma(1-alpha)) s^(1-alpha)
=> m(t) ~ m0/(c Gamma(1-alpha) Gamma(alpha)) t^-alpha   (POWER-LAW tail)

==> non-Markovian signature = power-law tail; Markov = exponential.

--- 4. stretched exponential (Weibull) is NOT power-law ---
m(t) = m0 e^{-(t/tau)^beta}: decays FASTER than any power law at large t.
=> discriminating power vs stretched-exponential needs the far tail.
